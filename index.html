🍴 Chow Time

Seat · Order · Cook · Serve · Clean

Run a small restaurant. Lay out stoves, tables, and chairs; hire cooks; and serve hungry customers before they rage out. Every night brings a surprise event — assign a chef to deal with it, and plan for tomorrow's forecast.

Quick Rules

  • You start with 100 reputation ★. Every angry customer costs 15; hitting daily quota earns 1 back.
  • Between days, a random event needs a chef — assigning one costs you that chef the next day.
  • Tomorrow's forecast hints at crowd size, food bias, or tip mood — plan around it.
  • Right-click to remove furniture or cancel a Move pickup.
